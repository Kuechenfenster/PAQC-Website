Who we are

A consultancy built for regulatory complexity

PAQ-Consultancy was founded on a simple observation: regulation is becoming the hardest part of bringing a product to market. Standards shift, borders multiply requirements, and a single missed detail can stop a shipment — or a launch.

We exist to make that complexity navigable. Our work spans three interconnected disciplines — international product compliance, negotiation training, and Industry 5.0 transformation — delivered with the same principle: precision first, jargon last.

The next shift

Industry 5.0 puts people back at the centre of the machine

The fourth industrial revolution automated. The fifth collaborates. Industry 5.0 asks a different question — not what can machines do instead of people, but what can they do alongside them. Personalised products, resilient and sustainable operations, and technology that amplifies human judgement rather than replacing it.
