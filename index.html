The problem we solve

Inspection catches defects. It doesn’t prevent them.

Most sourcing offices spend their quality budget at the end of the line — inspectors, re-tests, re-works, air freight. The defect was built weeks earlier: in the material choice, the tooling, the supplier’s process, or a regulation nobody read in time.

We move quality to where it is made. Fewer surprises, fewer tests, fewer sleepless nights before a retailer audit.

Sound familiar?

  • A major retailer’s technical team rejected your shipment — and you found out from the customer.
  • Your test budget grows every year, but so do the complaints.
  • PPWR, Digital Product Passport, PFAS and CMR limits are coming, and nobody in the office owns the topic.
  • Your sourcing office needs a Head of Quality, but the business can’t carry a full-time senior salary.

Regulatory Radar

What’s changing — and when it hits your products

  • Packaging & Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR)

    Recyclability, minimisation and labelling rules for every consumer product shipped into the EU.

  • Digital Product Passport (DPP)

    Product-level data requirements starting with priority categories; sourcing offices will need the data pipeline in place.

  • PFAS and CMR restrictions

    Expanding substance limits on toys, cosmetics, textiles and food-contact articles.

  • General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR)

    Responsible-person and traceability duties for all non-food consumer products in the EU.
