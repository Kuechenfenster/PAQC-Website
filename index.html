Our Industry 5.0 approach

Technology that works alongside people

Industry 5.0 builds on connected manufacturing with a stronger focus on people, sustainability, and resilience.

For PAQ-Consultancy, this means integrating systems and AI in ways that support human expertise—not simply adding more automation. The goal is to help teams access relevant knowledge, understand their operations, and respond to changing requirements.

  • Human-centricDesign technology around the people who operate, manage, and improve your processes.
  • SustainableConsider resource use and environmental responsibilities as part of transformation planning.
  • ResilientSupport operations that can adapt to changing production needs, information, and requirements.
  • CollaborativeConnect human judgement with manufacturing systems and AI-assisted knowledge.

AIG & RAG solutions

Your knowledge. Accessible where work happens.

Valuable organisational knowledge is often spread across documents, departments, and disconnected systems.

Our AIG and RAG solutions help bring that information into everyday workflows. RAG retrieves relevant material from connected knowledge sources to inform AI-generated responses, helping teams work with organisational context rather than relying only on a model’s general knowledge.

  • Connect your knowledge — bring together selected procedures, technical documentation, and compliance references.
  • Make information easier to use — help teams locate relevant information through natural-language questions.
  • Keep people in control — AI assists understanding and decision-making, with human review for important operational and compliance decisions.
