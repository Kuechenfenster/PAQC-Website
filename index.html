Who we are

Regulatory certainty, built like a machine

PAQ-Consultancy Ltd is the system-compliance practice for manufacturers and brands who need more than advice — they need architecture. We combine deep expertise in international product compliance, negotiation training, and Industry 5.0 transformation — underpinned by our AI knowledge base, into one coherent discipline we call System Compliance.

Every engagement runs on the same principle: knowledge captured once, organised properly, and leveraged everywhere — from audit trails in Dongguan to negotiation tables in Milan. Our proprietary PAQ system turns compliance from a cost centre into continuous infrastructure.

The next shift

Industry 5.0 is not more technology. It is technology that respects people.

The fourth industrial revolution optimised machines. The fifth puts the human being back at the centre — and demands that sustainability and resilience are engineered in, not bolted on. That is precisely where compliance knowledge becomes a strategic asset.

  • Human well-beingEvery system we design keeps the operator, the engineer and the customer at the centre of the loop.
  • SustainabilityCircular, compliant by design — materials, processes and documentation that stand up to CSRD and beyond.
  • ResilienceKnowledge doesn't retire with employees; PAQ's AI layer makes institutional memory permanent.
  • Human–machine collaborationWe deploy technology where it amplifies judgement, never where it replaces accountability.
