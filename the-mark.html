Our Industry 5.0 approach

Technology that works alongside people

Industry 5.0 builds on connected manufacturing with a stronger focus on people, sustainability, and resilience.

For PAQ-Consultancy, this means integrating systems and AI in ways that support human expertise — not simply adding more automation. The goal is to help teams access relevant knowledge, understand their operations, and respond to changing requirements.

  • Human-centric. Design technology around the people who operate, manage, and improve your processes.
  • Sustainable. Consider resource use and environmental responsibilities as part of transformation planning.
  • Resilient. Support operations that can adapt to changing production needs, information, and requirements.
  • Collaborative. Connect human judgement with manufacturing systems and AI-assisted knowledge.

Our AIG and RAG solutions bring procedures, technical documentation, and compliance references into everyday workflows: retrieval-augmented systems answer natural-language questions from your own knowledge base, so teams work with organisational context rather than a model’s general knowledge — with human review for important operational and compliance decisions. Deployed locally or with cloud support, matched to your environment, data-handling requirements, and intended use.
